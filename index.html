Retail BI & Financial Modeling

Global Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard

Tech Stack: Power BI • DAX • Power Query • Data Modeling

Business Objective

Multi-regional retail enterprises struggle to identify profit leaks, underperforming product categories, and shipping overheads across thousands of transactions.

Implementation & Architecture

  • ETL Pipeline via Power Query: Automated data transformation of complex sales logs, cleansed duplicate records, and established clean date dimensions.
  • Star Schema Modeling: Created clean relationships between Fact_Sales and Dim_Customers, Dim_Products, Dim_Geography.
  • DAX Formulations: Engineered measures for Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth, and Shipping Delay Penalties.
  • Self-Service Slicers: Dynamic filters for Region, Segment, and Category enabling executive drill-down.

Business Impact

Enabled management to immediately pinpoint unprofitable sub-categories and negotiate optimized shipping terms in loss-making territories.
